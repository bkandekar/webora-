Refund Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how deposits, payments and cancellations are handled for projects booked through Webora. This is a template — please review and adapt it to match your actual business terms.

Project Deposits

Most projects require an upfront deposit to begin work, with the balance due at agreed milestones or upon completion, as outlined in your project quote.

Cancellations

If a project is cancelled before work begins, the deposit may be refunded minus any costs already incurred. Once design or development work has started, deposits are generally non-refundable.

Refund Eligibility

Refund requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis, taking into account the amount of work completed and time invested at the point of cancellation.

Non-Refundable Items

Third-party costs already incurred on your behalf — such as domain registration, premium plugins, stock assets or hosting fees — are non-refundable.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us with your project details and reason for the request. We aim to respond within a few business days.
